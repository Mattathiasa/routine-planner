Everything you need to optimize your daily Genshin routine

No cluttered menus. No bloated ad trackers. Just pure, fast, offline utilities built around the actual rhythms of the game.

Mathematical Resin Anchor

Zero battery drain. Never runs background loops or ticking services. Uses the exact 8-minute game regeneration formula projected from an immutable anchor timestamp.

  • Exact 160 (Condensed) & 200 (Cap) forecasts
  • Tracks Condensed, Fragile & Transient stacks

Multi-Server Reset Clocks

Daily reset in Genshin occurs at 04:00 server time. Routine Planner maps Europe (UTC+1), America (UTC-5), Asia (UTC+8), and TW/HK/MO into your exact local time zone.

  • Interactive 7-day schedule strip
  • Automatic domain open/close detection

3:1 Tier Material Aggregation

Stop guessing how many green drops convert to purple books. Our tiered alchemy calculator carries surplus upwards with the game's 3:1 ratio and factors in your active inventory.

  • Ascension 1 → 6 and Talents 1 → 10
  • "Most Needed Materials" aggregated view

Reliable Local Notifications

Never cap your resin during a busy day. Native Android exact alarms trigger at your chosen thresholds (e.g. 160 or 200) and alert you 24 hours before Transient Resin expires.

  • Works offline without internet connection
  • Graceful fallback for inexact battery modes

Dual SQLite Architecture

Built on Drift and SQLite. The catalog database is verified by SHA-256 for integrity, while your personal plan stays in your private user database. Export anytime to clean JSON.

  • Full JSON backup & restore support
  • Zero third-party analytics or surveillance

Optional HoYoLab Live Sync

Prefer automatic updates over manual sync? Connect your HoYoLab account with a single tap. Session cookies are kept in Android EncryptedSharedPreferences, never sent to external servers.

  • 100% optional, manual entry always supported
  • Strict on-device hardware security
