Rules that keep it usable

  • Ornament on frames, headers and tabs only. Numbers and labels stay plain.
  • One glowing element per view: the primary action or the current state.
  • All motion loops are decorative and stop under reduce-motion.
  • Gold on dark for emphasis; body text stays ivory at 4.5:1 or better.
  • Element colors always come with a glyph, never color alone.
